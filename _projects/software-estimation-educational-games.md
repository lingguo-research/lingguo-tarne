This project focuses on the design and development of six educational games for teaching software estimation. The games are based on SimSE and address different estimation activities and decision-making scenarios.

The project also investigates how educational games can support graduate-level and professional software engineering education.

**Research focus:** Software estimation · Software engineering education · Educational games · SimSE
